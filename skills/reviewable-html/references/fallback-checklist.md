# Noted house-template checklist

Use this only when no applicable brand skill or branded HTML template exists.

- All bracketed placeholders in the copied template are replaced or removed.
- The title states the artifact's subject, not a generic document type.
- The summary leads with the decision, recommendation, or current status.
- Each major section and review target has a stable, descriptive `id`.
- Evidence and assumptions are distinguishable from recommendations.
- Tables have a caption or nearby context, header cells, and no clipped columns.
- Links have descriptive labels and do not expose credentials or local-only
  absolute paths.
- The layout works at 1280px and 390px widths without horizontal page overflow.
- Focus indicators remain visible and text remains readable at 200% zoom.
- No external request is required for the core content or theme.
- Print output remains legible and does not split short cards unnecessarily.
